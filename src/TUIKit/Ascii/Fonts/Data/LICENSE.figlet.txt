TUIKit — Bundled ASCII-Art Font Attribution
===========================================

The .flf files in this directory are FIGlet and TOIlet fonts drawn from the
patorjk/figlet.js distribution (https://github.com/patorjk/figlet.js). Each is
included under its own font-author terms. None of the bundled fonts carry
restrictive licensing. All requested fonts cleared a header-text license scan
and are bundled below.

TUIKit itself is MIT-licensed. Font glyph data remains the property of the
respective font authors credited below.
