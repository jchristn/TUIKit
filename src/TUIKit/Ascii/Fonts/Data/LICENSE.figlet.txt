TUIKit — Bundled ASCII-Art Font Attribution
===========================================

The .flf files in this directory are FIGlet and TOIlet fonts drawn from the
patorjk/figlet.js distribution (https://github.com/patorjk/figlet.js). Each is
included under its own font-author terms. None of the bundled fonts carry
restrictive licensing. Every bundled font cleared a header-text license scan;
fonts that did not are listed in REMOVED.txt.

FateGate and Bleach are TheDraw (.tdf) fonts served by patorjk TAAG
(https://patorjk.com/software/taag/tdf-font-sets/). TheDraw font files carry
no license text. TUIKit converts them to FIGlet: CP437 characters are mapped to
Unicode, color attributes are dropped, and letter spacing and space width match
TAAG's plain-text TheDraw renderer. FateGate is the "FateGate Blu" variant; the
other color variants share its glyph shapes.

TUIKit itself is MIT-licensed. Font glyph data remains the property of the
respective font authors credited below.
