TUIKit — ASCII-Art Fonts Excluded From the Library
==================================================

Every candidate font was scanned for license text. A font is excluded when its
header asserts rights without granting permission to redistribute or modify.

TAAG name              Reason
---------              ------
Efti Wall              Header reads "This font is (c) Michel Eftimakis 1995" with
                       no permission grant. Excluded under the "when in doubt,
                       remove it" rule.

Consumers can still load any additional font they hold the rights to at
runtime via FigletFontLoader.
